**Subject** - A peaceful cabin in the woods surrounded by trees, flowers, and a small lake.

**Prompt A: Watercolor Painting**

Create a peaceful landscape painting using watercolor. The artwork should feature a small wooden cabin surrounded by tall trees, colorful wildflowers, distant mountains, and a calm lake in the foreground. Emphasize line, shape, color, texture, balance and unity. Use soft greens, blues, browns, and warm yellow tones to create a relaxing atmosphere. Use watercolor brushes, watercolor paper, and diluted pigments to create transparent washes, soft color gradients, and blended edges. Apply the wet-on-wet technique to create natural-looking trees, reflections, and a soft sky. Use layering to build depth while allowing some colors to blend and bleed naturally. Make the cabin the main focal point on the composition.

**Prompt B: Linocut Printmaking**

Create a peaceful landscape artwork using linocut printmaking. The artwork should feature the same small wooden cabin surrounded by tall trees, colorful wildflowers, distant mountains, and a calm lake in the foreground. Emphasize line, shape, contrast, texture, balance and unity. Use bold black and white areas with limited greens, blues, and warm brown tones. Use linoleum blocks, carving tools, printing ink, and paper to create the image. Apply the carving and relief printing processes to create bold outlines, strong shapes, and textured details. Use carved lines to show tree branches, grass, flowers, and the wooden cabin. Keep the cabin as the main focal point. Maintain the same composition as the watercolor version while creating a more graphic appearance with crisp edges and high contrast.  

**Elements of Principle of Design:**
- Line: Tree branches, grass, and cabin details.
- Shape: Cabin, trees, mountains and flowers.
- Color: Greens, blues, browns, and warm yellow.
- Texture: Tree bark, leaves, water and wood.
- Balance: Cabin positioned near the center with trees surrounding it.
- Emphasis: Cabin as the main focal point.
